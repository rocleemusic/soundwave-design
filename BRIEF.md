## Brand Voice

- **Register:** Refined, technical, confident — "research lab meets mix stage"
- **Tone:** Direct, no marketing bloat. Short sentences. No "solutions," "synergies," "leverage."
- **Not:** Warm/folksy. Not corporate-sterile.
- **Proof over claims:** Let credits and client work speak. State facts, not adjectives.

---

## Design Direction

- **Mode:** Dark-mode default (standard in game audio category)
- **Typography:** Geometric or neo-grotesque sans for display; neutral sans for body; mono for credits/specs
- **Motion:** Restrained — fade-in on scroll, hover ≤200ms. No scroll-jacking, no autoplay audio
- **Imagery:** Real studio/gear/team photography preferred. Avoid soundwave stock art.

### Color Tokens *(approved 2026-04-18)*
```
--bg:          #0C0B08   (near-black, warm undertone to match gold)
--surface:     #181510   (card/panel backgrounds)
--text:        #F5EFE0   (warm off-white — same temperature family as gold)
--text-muted:  #B4A282   (secondary text — AAA contrast on both bg and surface)
--accent:      #C9A55A   (primary gold — from logo S letterform)
--accent-light: #E0C07A  (hover state — lighter logo outline gold)
--accent-deep:  #8A6E3A  (bronze — tags, borders, secondary elements)
```
